# Repair Bond Oil

## What it is
A strengthening treatment oil that helps rebuild and protect hair weakened by heat, bleach, and chemical processing. It smooths the surface for shine while supporting the hair's internal bonds.

## Who it's for
Best for damaged, color-treated, and chemically processed hair (bleached, relaxed, or permed). Great for anyone fighting breakage and split ends.

## Key ingredients
- Bond-building Complex — helps reconnect broken bonds to reduce breakage.
- Argan Oil — adds slip, softness, and shine.
- Vitamin E — an antioxidant that conditions and protects.

## How to use
Apply 2–3 drops to damp mid-lengths and ends before styling, or smooth a drop over dry hair as a finishing shine. A little goes a long way.

## Ingredients & safety
Color-safe and bleach-safe. Sulfate-free and paraben-free. Vegan and cruelty-free.

## Common questions
**Is the Repair Bond Oil safe for color-treated or bleached hair?**
Yes. It is specifically formulated for color-treated, bleached, and chemically processed hair, and it is color-safe.

**Will it make fine hair greasy?**
Not if you use 1–2 drops on the ends only. Fine hair should avoid the roots.

**How often should I use it?**
Daily on ends is fine, or use as a pre-style treatment a few times a week for heavier repair.
