# Air-Dry Styling Gel

**Category:** Styling — Gel
**Price:** $20 (8 fl oz)
**Rating:** 4.7 / 5 (980 reviews)

## What it is
A flexible-hold styling gel for soft, crunch-free definition on wash-and-go days. It locks in shape and fights frizz while keeping hair touchable.

## Who it's for
Best for wavy, curly, and coily hair. Ideal for anyone who wants definition without a stiff or sticky finish.

## Key ingredients
- Flaxseed Gel — natural, flexible hold.
- Marshmallow Root — slip and softness that eases definition.
- Glycerin — locks in moisture for springy, hydrated curls.

## How to use
Smooth over wet hair in sections, scrunch to encourage curl, and air-dry. Once fully dry, scrunch out any light cast for a soft finish.

## Ingredients & safety
Color-safe and alcohol-free, so it won't dry out hair or cause flaking. Vegan and cruelty-free.

## Common questions
**Is the Air-Dry Styling Gel safe for color-treated hair?**
Yes. It is color-safe and alcohol-free, so it styles without drying or fading color-treated hair.

**Can I layer it with the Define Curl Cream?**
Yes. Apply the Curl Cream first for moisture and definition, then the gel on top for longer-lasting hold.

**Will it flake?**
No. It is formulated to avoid the flaking that drying, alcohol-based gels can cause.
