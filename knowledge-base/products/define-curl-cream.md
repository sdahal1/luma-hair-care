## What it is
A lightweight, curl-defining cream that shapes bouncy, frizz-free curls and coils without stiffness or buildup. It hydrates while it styles, so curls stay soft and touchable through the day.

## Who it's for
Best for curly, coily, and wavy hair. Works on everything from loose waves to tight coils. Safe for color-treated hair.

## Key ingredients
- Shea Butter — seals in moisture and tames frizz.
- Flaxseed Extract — provides soft, flexible curl hold.
- Glycerin — draws in hydration to keep curls springy.

## How to use
Rake through soaking-wet hair, scrunch upward toward the scalp, then air-dry or diffuse on low. Avoid touching curls while they dry to reduce frizz.

## Ingredients & safety
Color-safe. Sulfate-free, silicone-free, and paraben-free. Vegan and cruelty-free.

## Common questions
**Is the Define Curl Cream safe for color-treated hair?**
Yes. It is color-safe and free of sulfates and silicones, so it won't strip or dull color-treated or highlighted hair.

**Will it make my hair crunchy?**
No. It gives soft, flexible definition. If you notice any light cast after air-drying, scrunch it out with your hands.

**Can I use it on dry hair for refresh?**
Yes. Emulsify a small amount with a little water and scrunch into curls to revive them between wash days.
