## What it is
A weightless thermal protection mist that shields hair from heat damage up to 450°F. It guards against dryness and breakage while adding a light, smooth finish before styling.

## Who it's for
Best for all hair types, including fine and color-treated hair. Anyone who blow-dries, flat-irons, or curls should use it.

## Key ingredients
- Heat-activated Polymers — form a protective shield against heat.
- Bamboo Extract — supports strength and shine.
- Provitamin B5 (Panthenol) — adds moisture and softness.

## How to use
Mist evenly over damp or dry hair before blow-drying, flat-ironing, or curling. Comb through to distribute, then style as usual.

## Ingredients & safety
Color-safe, non-sticky, and lightweight. Vegan and cruelty-free.

## Common questions
**Is the Heat Shield Mist safe for color-treated hair?**
Yes. It is color-safe and helps protect color-treated hair from the fading and dryness that heat styling can cause.

**What temperature does it protect against?**
It provides thermal protection up to 450°F (232°C).

**Will it weigh down fine hair?**
No. It is a lightweight mist designed to protect without buildup, so it suits fine hair well.
