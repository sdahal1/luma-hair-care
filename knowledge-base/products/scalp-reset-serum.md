## What it is
A soothing pre-wash scalp serum that balances oil, calms irritation, and creates a healthy foundation for growth. It targets the scalp specifically rather than the hair strands.

## Who it's for
Best for oily or sensitive scalps, and suitable for all hair types. Helpful for flakiness, itchiness, or product buildup at the roots.

## Key ingredients
- Niacinamide — supports a balanced, comfortable scalp.
- Tea Tree — clarifies and helps control excess oil.
- Peptide Blend — nourishes the scalp environment.

## How to use
Massage into a dry or damp scalp about 10 minutes before washing, then shampoo as usual. Use 2–3 times per week.

## Ingredients & safety
Available in a fragrance-free option. Sulfate-free and paraben-free. Vegan and cruelty-free.

## Common questions
**Is the Scalp Reset Serum safe for color-treated hair?**
Yes. It is applied to the scalp and rinsed out during washing, and it is color-safe.

**Can sensitive scalps use it?**
Yes. Choose the fragrance-free option and start with twice a week. Patch test first if you have very reactive skin.

**Does it help with growth?**
It supports a healthy scalp environment, which is the foundation for healthy growth. It is not a medical treatment.
