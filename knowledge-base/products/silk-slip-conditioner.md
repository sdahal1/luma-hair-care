## What it is
A deeply nourishing conditioner with rich slip that makes detangling effortless and leaves hair soft, smooth, and shiny. It restores moisture after cleansing without weighing hair down.

## Who it's for
Best for dry, curly, and color-treated hair. Anyone who struggles with tangles or post-wash dryness will benefit.

## Key ingredients
- Slip-rich Conditioning Blend — melts through knots for easy detangling.
- Shea Butter — softens and seals in moisture.
- Hydrolyzed Quinoa — lightweight protein that supports smoothness and strength.

## How to use
Work through clean, wet hair from mid-lengths to ends, comb through to detangle, leave on for 1–2 minutes, then rinse. Pair with Hydra Wash Shampoo.

## Ingredients & safety
Color-safe and silicone-free. Vegan and cruelty-free.

## Common questions
**Is the Silk Slip Conditioner safe for color-treated hair?**
Yes. It is color-safe and silicone-free, so it conditions without dulling or coating color-treated hair.

**Can I use it as a leave-in?**
It is designed as a rinse-out conditioner. For leave-in slip, use a small amount on the ends and don't rinse fully.

**Will silicone-free still detangle well?**
Yes. The slip comes from conditioning agents rather than silicones, so you get easy detangling without buildup.
