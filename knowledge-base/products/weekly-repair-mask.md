## What it is
An intensive deep-conditioning mask that restores moisture and softness to dry, frizzy, and damaged hair. A weekly treatment that leaves hair noticeably smoother and more resilient.

## Who it's for
Best for dry, frizzy, damaged, and color-treated hair. Ideal for hair stressed by heat styling, bleach, or the elements.

## Key ingredients
- Murumuru Butter — rich, deep moisture for parched strands.
- Hyaluronic Acid — attracts and holds water for lasting hydration.
- Rice Protein — strengthens and smooths the hair surface.

## How to use
Apply to clean, damp hair from mid-lengths to ends once a week, leave on for 5–10 minutes, then rinse well. For extra repair, cover with a cap and add gentle heat.

## Ingredients & safety
Color-safe and silicone-free. Vegan and cruelty-free.

## Common questions
**Is the Weekly Repair Mask safe for color-treated hair?**
Yes. It is color-safe and silicone-free, making it a good weekly moisture boost for color-treated and damaged hair.

**How often should I use it?**
Once a week is ideal. Very damaged hair can use it up to twice a week.

**Can fine hair use it?**
Yes, but apply to the ends only and rinse thoroughly so it doesn't weigh hair down.
