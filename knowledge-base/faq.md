# Luma Hair Care — General FAQ

## About our products
**Are Luma products vegan and cruelty-free?**
Yes. Every Luma product is 100% vegan and cruelty-free, and never tested on animals.

**Are your products safe for color-treated hair?**
Yes. The full Luma range is color-safe. Our cleansers are sulfate-free so they won't strip or fade color.

**What do you leave out of your formulas?**
We formulate without sulfates, parabens, and (in most products) silicones. Individual product pages list specifics.

**Where are Luma products made?**
Luma products are formulated and made in the USA with globally sourced clean ingredients.

## Shipping
**How much is shipping?**
Standard shipping is free on orders over $45. Orders under $45 have a flat $5 shipping fee.

**How fast will my order arrive?**
Standard shipping is 3–5 business days. Expedited 2-day shipping is available at checkout.

**Is shipping carbon-neutral?**
Yes. We offset shipping emissions on every order.

## Returns & refunds
**What is your return policy?**
We offer a 30-day happiness guarantee. If you're not satisfied, you can return a product within 30 days of delivery for a full refund, even if it's been opened and tried.

**How do I start a return or refund?**
Email hello@lumahair.com or use the support chat with your order number. We'll send a prepaid return label.

**How long do refunds take?**
Refunds are issued to your original payment method within 5–7 business days after we receive the return.

## Subscriptions

Yes. Subscribe to any product for 15% off and free shipping, and change or cancel anytime.
