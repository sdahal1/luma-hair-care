# Luma Hair-Type & Concern Guide

Use this guide to match customers to the right routine. All Luma products are color-safe, vegan, and cruelty-free.

## Color-treated hair
Goal: clean gently and protect color from fading.
- Wash: Hydra Wash Shampoo (sulfate-free, color-safe)
- Condition: Silk Slip Conditioner
- Protect: Heat Shield Mist before any heat styling
- Repair: Repair Bond Oil on ends if bleached or highlighted

## Dry or frizzy hair
Goal: add and seal in moisture.
- Wash: Hydra Wash Shampoo
- Condition: Silk Slip Conditioner
- Weekly: Weekly Repair Mask
- Style: Define Curl Cream or Air-Dry Styling Gel to lock in moisture

## Curly and coily hair
Goal: definition without crunch or frizz.
- Define: Define Curl Cream on soaking-wet hair
- Hold: Air-Dry Styling Gel layered over the cream
- Hydration: Silk Slip Conditioner and the Weekly Repair Mask
- Refresh: emulsify a little Curl Cream with water between washes

## Damaged or over-processed hair
Goal: rebuild strength and reduce breakage.
- Treat: Repair Bond Oil before styling
- Weekly: Weekly Repair Mask
- Protect: Heat Shield Mist before heat
- Wash gently: Hydra Wash Shampoo

## Fine hair
Goal: protection and definition without weight.
- Protect: Heat Shield Mist (lightweight, no buildup)
- Repair: Repair Bond Oil, 1–2 drops on ends only
- Avoid heavy butters at the roots; keep masks to the ends

## Oily or sensitive scalp
Goal: balance the scalp and control excess oil.
- Treat: Scalp Reset Serum as a pre-wash, 2–3 times a week
- Wash: Hydra Wash Shampoo
- Choose the fragrance-free Scalp Reset Serum for sensitive scalps
